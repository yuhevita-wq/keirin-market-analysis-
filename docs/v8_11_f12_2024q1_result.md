5. FINAL was also poor: 21 bets, -18,010 yen, ROI 26.79%. The provisional H_BALANCED rule was not supported by this Q1 result.
6. Race-type adaptation is therefore useful as a framework, but the initial SPECIAL and FINAL rules should not be retained as-is.
